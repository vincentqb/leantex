\section{Numbers}
The identity
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:idty}
  a + b = b + a
\end{equation}
is Equation~\eqref{eq:idty}; the starred form
\begin{equation*}
  c = d
\end{equation*}
takes no number, and
\begin{equation}
  e = f \nonumber
\end{equation}
opts out, so the next one
\begin{equation}
  g = h
\end{equation}
is second.
