An invented survey of page apparatus opens here\footnote{the first
note, standing at the foot of page one} and continues with plain
running text about nothing in particular, set only to give the page a
body worth footnoting.

Synthetic paragraphs follow, each one an invented remark about layout
that carries no meaning beyond its own length, so the article grows
toward a second page at an even pace without naming any real subject.

A measured cadence of made-up sentences keeps the flow moving, and the
words repeat their gentle nonsense in slightly different orders so that
no two lines break in quite the same place on the way down the page.

The rhythm of the block continues with more invented prose, a steady
column of filler whose only purpose is to press the following claims
far enough down to meet the bottom of the first page eventually.

Another paragraph of the same invented kind follows the last, saying
nothing new at a comfortable length, and the page fills a little more
with every repeated variation on this deliberately empty theme.

Filler prose keeps arriving in even paragraphs, and the reader who has
come this far already knows that nothing here means anything at all,
which is exactly what a synthetic fixture is supposed to say.

The column continues its patient descent with one more block of
invented sentences, holding the same measure and the same voice while
the remaining space above the margin quietly runs out below it.

Yet another stretch of manufactured text follows, neither longer nor
shorter than its neighbours, so the break between the pages falls
wherever the metrics put it rather than where anyone planned.

More sentences of the same shape arrive on schedule, and the paragraph
count climbs toward the point where the first page must close and the
second page must open underneath a fresh top margin.

The filler marches on with its even tread, one invented clause after
another, each line a plain rehearsal of the line before it with only
the smallest changes of wording to keep the breaker honest.

A further installment of the same manufactured prose settles into
place beneath the last, spending its three or four lines on nothing at
all while the page's remaining depth continues its steady shrink.

The next paragraph repeats the exercise with a straight face, laying
one more even course of invented sentences onto the column and moving
the story of this page no distance whatsoever in any direction.

Still the filler comes, patient and interchangeable, a block of words
whose entire contribution is its height, followed by a gap, followed
by the promise of another block exactly like it directly below.

Another course of synthetic clauses lands here in the same voice, and
the measure carries it without complaint, one more increment in the
long even descent toward the foot of the opening page.

The paragraphs continue to arrive like bricks from the same mould,
each one set flush on the last, and the wall they build has now grown
tall enough that the first page must be close to full.

One more block of deliberately hollow prose takes its place in the
column, neither hurrying nor delaying the break, simply occupying its
few lines the way every previous block has occupied its own.

And still the invented text runs on, sentence after sentence of
carefully meaningless filler, so that whatever falls after this point
must surely land beyond the boundary of the opening page.

A boundary claim arrives late on its page\footnote{the boundary note,
moved whole with its line} and keeps its note beside it, wherever the
break between the pages happens to fall.

The closing stretch of the article resumes the invented voice for a
few more paragraphs, so that the final claims stand well inside the
second page rather than hanging at its very top edge.

One more paragraph of deliberate nonsense holds the measure steady and
gives the second page a body of its own, in the same even voice the
first page used from its opening line.

A final remark takes a chosen number\footnote[12]{the chosen note,
numbered without stepping the counter} before the article closes on
its own quiet terms.
