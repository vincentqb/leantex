% Synthetic fixture: a running head over a deliberately tight top margin.
% The half margin cannot hold the head's line, so the head must reserve a
% band out of the body area (headBandFor / Geom.bodyTop) or it collides
% with the first line of body ink. Invented content only.
\documentclass{article}

\page{
  size = a5,
  vmargin = 14pt,
  hmargin = 0.6in,
}

\runninghead{Invented Field Notes \hfill p. \pagenumber}

\assert{ pages == 1 }

\begin{document}

\section{Under a tight margin}

The running head above this page sits in a top margin far too small to
hold it, so the engine reserves a band below the margin and the body
starts under the head's ink, never on it.

The first line of this paragraph is the one a missing reservation would
have collided with, which is exactly why this fixture exists.

\end{document}
