Example Icons (ExampleIcons-Regular.ttf) is an invented icon face built
for the leantex test corpus by scripts/gen-test-icons.py: five simple
geometric shapes drawn in that script, no outlines taken from any existing
font. The codepoints (U+F062, U+F08C, U+F09B, U+F0E0, U+F19D) follow the
Font Awesome 5 Free assignments as published in the fontawesome5 LaTeX
package's mapping table, so the fixtures can exercise the engine's
fontawesome5 spellings hermetically.
