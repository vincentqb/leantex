\section{Formulas}

Inline math sits inside its sentence: the map $f(x) = -x^2 + 3$ keeps its
leading sign tight against the variable, a function name stays upright as
in $\sin x \approx x - x^3$, scripts nest to the scriptscript floor in
$x^{y^z}$, and a pair stacks in $a_i^2 + b^{n}_{m}$. The paren form
\(2\pi r\) is the same inline math.

\[ \sum_i \alpha_i x^i \le \Gamma(n) \cdot \prod_j \beta_j \]

The paragraph continues after the display. Fractions nest as
$\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{n}}$, a radical takes an index in $\sqrt[3]{x + 1}$,
and a grown pair covers a tall fraction:

\[ \left( \frac{a + b}{c} \right)^2 = \frac{a^2 + 2ab + b^2}{c^2} \]

A big operator carries its limits below and above in display style,

\[ \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}, \qquad \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1, \]

while inline $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i$ keeps them beside. Alignment points share
one x per column:

\begin{align*}
  2 + 4 + \cdots + 2n &= n(n+1) \\
  f(x) &= x^2 - 1 \\
       &= (x-1)(x+1)
\end{align*}

\begin{gather*}
  a + b = c \\
  x^2 + y^2 = z^2
\end{gather*}

A matrix is an array between grown delimiters, its cells in text style:

\[ M = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 & x \\ x' & \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right] \]

Accents place by the face's attachment points: $\hat{x} + \bar{y} +
\vec{v} + \tilde{a} + \dot{p} + \ddot{q}$, a taller base lifts its mark in
$\hat{H}$, and $\overline{x + y}$ draws its rule.

$\overset{?}{=}$ stays text: what this slice does not model keeps its
source and a warning that names it.

An alphabet remaps its letters through the Mathematical Alphanumeric
block, Letterlike holes included: $\mathbb{R}^{d}$ and $\mathbb{C}$ are
double-struck, $\mathbf{v} + \boldsymbol{\beta}$ set bold, and
$\mathrm{Err}(p, q)$ is upright. A construction stands as a script's
argument in $V^\text{null}$ and $2^\frac{1}{2}$.

\begin{equation*}
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
\end{equation*}
