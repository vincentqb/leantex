% Synthetic fixture: LaTeX's text and colour commands used inside math.
% Every formula is invented. Ships its own faces so the checkout renders as
% a function of the checkout. What this fixture pins is the recovery
% boundary: the constructs below render as mathematics, and the one that
% does not degrades to its text content — never to its markup.
\documentclass{article}
\fonts{ dir = "fonts", body = "Source Serif Pro", math = "Fira Math" }

\begin{document}

\section{Text Inside Math}

A text-style command resolves to the alphabet it means, so $\textbf{u}$ is
the upright bold letter and $\textit{v}$ the italic one, exactly as
$\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathit{v}$ set them. A colour is presentation the math
list does not carry, so $\textcolor{ink}{w}$ renders its letter and the
colour is named as lost. A word stands upright inside a formula:
$\text{gain} + \text{loss}$, a brace group inside one is grouping alone in
$\text{a{b}c}$, and a symbol inside one contributes its scalar in
$\text{first\ldots last}$.

An alignment carries all of it at once:

\begin{align*}
  \textcolor{ink}{\textbf{\sum_k}} a_k &= b \\
                                    &= \text{\textbf{bound} above}
\end{align*}

What the engine still does not model degrades to content: $\overset{?}{=}$
sets its characters and names the construct, and no control sequence
reaches the page.
