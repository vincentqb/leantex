# source: etoolbox manual §2 (\newrobustcmd, \robustify), §3 (hooks and
# patching), §3.5 (boolean flags). TeX conditionals resolve from the
# document's own definitions (N0114).
# The hooks are one deferral mechanism, a row each: \AtBeginDocument replays
# its body at \begin{document}, \AtEndPreamble at the end of the preamble.
# A body carrying content therefore lands in the document (the \AtBeginDocument
# row witnesses text appearing in the body), and one carrying a declaration
# still reaches the preamble — the \AtEndPreamble row names two commands
# because a deferral with an empty body has no effect to witness, and text is
# not preamble material at that point.
body impl \ifdefined\nope a\else b\fi
pre impl \AtBeginDocument{x}
pre impl \AtEndPreamble{\newgeometry{textwidth=396pt}}
pre refuse:W0301 \newrobustcmd{\zz}[1]{#1}
pre refuse:W0301 \robustify{\emph}
pre refuse:W0301 \newbool{mybool}
pre refuse:W0301 \newbool{mybool}\setbool{mybool}{true}
pre refuse:W0301 \patchcmd{\emph}{a}{b}{}{}
pre refuse:E0111 \apptocmd{\emph}{x}{}{}
